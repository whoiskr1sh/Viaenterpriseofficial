Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

You can track your order by:

  • Going to My Orders page and clicking on your order
  • Using the tracking number sent to your email
  • Calling our support team with your order ID

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Net Banking
  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • Digital Wallets (Paytm, Amazon Pay)

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel your order if it hasn't been shipped yet. Go to My Orders and click the "Cancel Order" button. If the order has already been shipped, you can return it after delivery.

What are your shipping charges?

Our shipping charges are:

  • Free shipping on orders above ₹999
  • ₹99 for orders below ₹999
  • Express delivery: ₹199 (1-2 days)
  • Same-day delivery: ₹299 (available in select cities)

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location:

  • Metro cities: 2-3 business days
  • Other cities: 3-5 business days
  • Remote areas: 5-7 business days
  • Express delivery: 1-2 business days

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items:

  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Free returns for defective or wrong items
  • Return shipping charges apply for size/color exchanges
  • Refunds processed within 5-7 business days

How do I return an item?

To return an item:

  1. Go to My Orders and select the item
  2. Click "Return Item" and select reason
  3. Schedule a pickup or drop at our center
  4. Pack the item securely with original tags
  5. Hand over to our delivery partner

How do I create an account?

Creating an account is easy:

  1. Click on the "Login" icon in the top right
  2. Select "Create New Account"
  3. Enter your email, phone, and password
  4. Verify your email/phone number
  5. Start shopping with exclusive member benefits!

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click "Forgot Password?"
  3. Enter your registered email
  4. Check your email for reset link
  5. Create a new password

The website is not loading properly. What should I do?

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Check your internet connection
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily
  • Contact our technical support if issues persist

I'm having trouble placing an order. What should I do?

If you're having trouble with checkout:

  • Ensure all required fields are filled correctly
  • Check if your payment method is valid
  • Try using a different payment method
  • Clear your cart and try again
  • Contact support for immediate assistance
